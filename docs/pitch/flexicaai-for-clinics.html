Front desk

The diary that argues back.

It knows each doctor's hours, their days off and how many patients they will see. A booking that breaks one of those is refused as you make it — not discovered at the counter.

Booked out of hours on purpose?

Mark the visit as a one-off and the hours check steps aside — leave and the daily cap still apply. Agreeing to come in at 6pm is not the same as agreeing to work through your holiday.

This is the slide the front desk cares about. The point is not "we have a calendar" — everyone has a calendar. The point is that the calendar refuses the booking that would have caused an argument later.

The out-of-hours exception matters to owners: the software bends where a clinic really bends, and holds where it should not.

Trust

Patient records are not a shared folder.

Everyone signs in as themselves and sees only what their role allows. You decide who sees clinical notes, who takes money, who can change a price.

Only the author signsA draft note can only be approved by the clinician who dictated it.
Access by roleThe front desk sees no clinical notes unless you grant it.
Nothing is hard-deletedDeleted items go to Trash and can be restored.
Every action is loggedWho opened a record, who changed it, and when.

This slide answers a question they may be too polite to ask: "can my receptionist read everything?"

The audit trail is the one to press if the clinic has ever had a dispute about a missing payment or an altered record. Nothing is deleted, and every view is recorded.

Your treatment list, your prices, your arrangement with each doctor. Nothing here assumes a clinic shaped like anyone else's.

This is the objection-handling slide: "our clinic is different." Agree with them, then show that the differences are settings rather than special cases.

Doctor shares usually get the most questions. The key fact: a share is calculated on money COLLECTED, not on what was billed — so the clinic never pays out on money it has not received.

Patient records are the most sensitive thing a clinic holds. They are kept on a server we run for you, encrypted in transit, backed up with the files that belong to them.
